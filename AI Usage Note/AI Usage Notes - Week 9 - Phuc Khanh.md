### **2. Enemy Navigation Footprint and Wall-Adjacent A* Implementation**

* **Tool:** Codex, GPT-5, OpenAI coding assistant.
* **Access time:** August 4, 2026.
* **Prompt used:**

```text
Apply Plan 14 to fix enemy A* navigation when the player stands close to a
wall.
```

* **Purpose of use:** To make enemy movement and A* use configurable feet-sized navigation footprints while keeping combat collision independent and preventing direct movement through walls after a failed search.
* **Content generated by AI:** Added per-enemy navigation profiles, separate damage and contact rectangles, optional path targets and explicit search statuses, wall-adjacent approach candidates, bounded multi-goal A*, complete sampled edge validation, real-position grid anchoring, per-search collision caching, navigation-based spawn validation, and collision/path debug drawing.
* **Independent work/validation:** I chose the requested player-like feet-box approach and retained different footprint sizes for Chaser, Ranger, Diver, and Boss so they can be calibrated independently. I also required unreachable searches to produce no player-facing movement direction rather than preserving the old direct fallback.
